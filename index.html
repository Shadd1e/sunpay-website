What SunPay Does

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Fund Your Wallet

Top up your SunCoins balance via card, bank transfer, USSD, or crypto. ₦1 = 1 SunCoin, always.

NFC Tap to Pay

Hold your card near any Sun Jade NFC terminal. SunCoins deducted instantly. No cash, no hassle.

QR Payments

Scan a merchant QR code to pay or show your own QR to receive SunCoins from anyone.

Buy Airtime & Data

Top up any Nigerian network — MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9Mobile — directly from your SunCoins balance.

Transfer SunCoins

Send SunCoins to anyone on SunPay using their unique SunPay ID. Instant. Free. No limits.

Pay Utility Bills

Electricity, cable TV, and more — pay any bill in seconds directly from your SunCoins wallet.
